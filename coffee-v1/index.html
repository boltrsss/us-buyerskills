I Was Tired of Complicated Diets — Then I Changed My Morning Routine

After years of meal plans, strict rules, and routines I could never stick with, I wanted to find something that could fit naturally into my everyday life.

▣ Updated September 2026
● By Laura M.

Eventually, I got tired of starting over every Monday with another new plan. Low carb. Zero snacks. Calorie tracking apps. Hours of Sunday meal prep. I already knew the rules. My problem was that I could never stick with them for long.

I’ve never been the kind of person who enjoys weighing food first thing in the morning or spending Sunday prepping meals for the entire week.

Yet, I kept forcing myself into the cycle. Apps, fad meal plans, mysterious powders, overly elaborate breakfasts, and a mental checklist of a hundred tiny rules to keep track of every day.

The first few days always went great. But then real life would kick in—a crazy day at work, a weekend trip, or just one of those mornings where you can't even process a sentence before your first cup of coffee.

Eventually, I realized: I didn’t need more willpower. I needed a routine that required less of it.

The Thought That Changed My Perspective

It happened during a pretty ordinary conversation.

I was venting to a close friend about how many times I had tried—and failed—to completely overhaul my lifestyle. She listened to my exhausting list of habits I was trying to force, paused, and asked:

“Why are you trying to reinvent your entire life all at once?”

“Maybe you don’t need a more complicated routine. Maybe you just need an easier one.”

I kept thinking about what she said.

Because no matter what diet plan I had abandoned over the years, one single habit remained 100% consistent every single morning: I drank my coffee.

At home, on my commute, or at my desk. The coffee was happening regardless.

So instead of adding another chore to my already packed morning, I decided to look for a solution that piggybacked on a habit I already loved.
