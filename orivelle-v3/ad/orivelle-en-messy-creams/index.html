The controlled applicator also makes it easier to reach smaller nails and areas that can be difficult to cover with a larger brush or fingertip.

For people who have abandoned nail-care products because the application was inconvenient, this small design change can make the routine considerably easier.

A nail-care routine does not need to be complicated to become part of your day.

Reason #4: It Helps You Focus on the New Nail Growing In

One of the most common reasons people give up on nail care is that they expect the existing nail to look completely different within a few days.

But nails grow gradually.

The older portion of a thick, rough or discolored-looking nail does not simply disappear overnight. It must continue growing forward as newer nail becomes visible near the base.

That is why it can be more useful to pay attention to the area closest to the cuticle rather than checking only the tip of the nail.

People using a consistent nail-care routine often monitor gradual changes such as:

  • Fresher-looking growth near the base
  • A smoother-looking nail surface
  • Reduced dryness or flaking
  • A more even-looking texture
  • A healthier overall appearance

Taking a photograph before beginning a routine can also make gradual changes easier to notice over time.

Orivelle is not designed around an unrealistic overnight transformation.
